A — Full Progression System

XP for every action → levels with titles → badges for milestones → weekly quests → global leaderboard. The most engaging loop but the most to build. Recommended.

Pros

  • Strongest daily retention
  • Every action feels rewarded
  • Viral leaderboard sharing

Cons

  • Needs balance tuning
  • Backend XP/level tracking

B — Badges + Streaks (No Leaderboard)

Milestone badges, daily scan streaks, weekly goals — all self-contained. No social comparison, no backend XP. Lighter build, still satisfying for solo collectors.

Pros

  • Fast to ship
  • No social infrastructure
  • Works offline

Cons

  • Lower long-term retention
  • No viral/social loop

C — Collection Score + Light Gamification

A single "CoinSeek Score" based on rarity and variety of your collection. Grows permanently, shows your all-time rank. Minimal maintenance, no time-limited content needed.

Pros

  • Very simple to build
  • Evergreen — no content upkeep

Cons

  • Weakest daily engagement
  • No active challenge hook
